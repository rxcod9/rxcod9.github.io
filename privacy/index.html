Privacy policy

Last updated: 9 August 2026

This policy explains what information this portfolio may process, why it is processed, and the choices available to visitors. The site does not sell personal information.

Information processed automatically

When you visit, hosting and security providers may process technical request information such as IP address, browser and device type, requested pages, timestamps, approximate location derived from IP, and diagnostic or security events. This information is used to deliver, protect, monitor, and troubleshoot the site.

Analytics and tag management

Google Analytics 4 and Google Tag Manager are used to understand page usage, navigation, traffic performance, and interactions such as opening chat. Google may process device, browser, usage, and approximate-location information. Advertising storage and advertising personalization are denied. Google Tag Manager is the single analytics loader, and Google Analytics does not load until you select Allow optional.

Privacy-minimized events may record project views, résumé viewing or downloading, successful résumé-link copying, meeting-link selection, chat opening, and outbound social selection. These events do not include résumé content, clipboard content, chat messages, email addresses, or scheduling details.

Session insights

Crazy Egg may collect interaction information such as page views, clicks, scrolling, viewport size, and aggregated session behavior. This is used to identify usability and content problems. The portfolio does not intentionally send contact-form content to Crazy Egg.

Chat and meeting services

Tawk.to provides an optional live-chat service and does not load until you allow optional services. Loading or using chat may allow Tawk.to to process cookies or similar identifiers, IP address, device and browser information, message content, and technical information needed to operate the chat and retain its history. If you choose to book a meeting, Calendly processes the details you submit under its own privacy practices. Do not send passwords, payment information, or other sensitive information through chat.

Contact form and email

The contact form and public email address are currently disabled. The site does not collect contact-form submissions. You may initiate contact through Tawk.to chat or the Calendly meeting link.

Location map

The contact page embeds Google Maps to show Ghaziabad, India. Loading the map connects your browser to Google, which may receive technical and usage information under Google's own privacy practices.

Storage, cookies, and retention

The site stores theme preference and your privacy preference in browser local storage. Third-party services may use cookies or similar technologies only after you opt in. Each provider determines its own retention period. Portfolio operational information is retained only as long as reasonably needed for security, diagnostics, analytics, or responding to a chat.

Your choices

Optional analytics, session-insight, and live-chat integrations load only after you select Allow optional services. Selecting Necessary only keeps them disabled. You can reopen Privacy choices from the footer at any time; withdrawing consent reloads the page after disabling optional integrations.

External links and referrer privacy

Company and project links open with a no-referrer policy. The destination should not receive this portfolio's address through the standard HTTP Referer header. A destination may still learn about a visit through information it collects directly after its page loads.

Data requests and policy changes

You may request access to or deletion of information supplied through chat by starting a new Tawk.to conversation and identifying the relevant interaction. This policy may be updated when site features or providers change; the date above identifies the latest revision.
